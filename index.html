01
Why this matters
→

Economics works better when women are part of shaping it.

Women remain underrepresented across economics — from undergraduate study and graduate training to senior academic, policy and leadership positions.

IEA-WE examines the structural barriers behind this gap and supports evidence-based approaches to creating a more inclusive profession.

A more diverse economics profession benefits everyone. Different perspectives strengthen research, policy and the questions economists ask.
A global challenge requires a global response. IEA-WE brings together economists, researchers and institutions across regions to understand what works.

06
About IEA-WE
→

Advancing equity and excellence in economics.

The International Economic Association launched IEA-WE as a multi-year global initiative to strengthen the presence and influence of women in the economics profession.

Through research, collaboration and advocacy, the initiative works to expand the pipeline of women economists, remove structural barriers to their advancement, and amplify their voices in shaping global economic discourse.
