The project

An editorial bridge between research and the real world.

Launched in 2023, Amplifying Voices set out to broaden who gets to shape global policy conversations. What began as a small editorial initiative has grown into IEA-WE's primary mechanism for making research accessible to policymakers, journalists, and the public — one carefully edited, carefully placed article at a time.

Nearly every piece published features an author based in Africa, Asia, or Latin America and the Caribbean — regions where women economists remain structurally underrepresented in the outlets that shape policy debate.

Run as a one-person editorial operation by Navika Mehta, the project pairs hands-on mentoring with strategic media partnerships and full-cycle publication support.

The people behind the project

Amplifying Voices brings together women economists from across Africa, Asia, and Latin America and the Caribbean, helping their research reach the audiences and platforms shaping global economic debate.

Amplifying Voices Partnerships

Research that travels further.

Strategic partnerships help connect women economists with the platforms and audiences that shape international policy debate.

Project Syndicate

Project Syndicate produces and delivers original, high-quality commentaries to a global audience. Featuring contributions from political leaders, policymakers, scholars, business leaders and civic activists, it provides news media and their readers with analysis and insight.

VoxEU

VoxEU is CEPR's policy portal, established to promote research-based policy analysis and commentary by leading economists. Its audience includes economists working in the public sector, private sector, academia and media, as well as students of economics.

VoxDev

VoxDev is a platform for economists, policymakers, practitioners, donors, the private sector and others interested in development. Expert contributors provide accessible commentary, analysis and evidence on a wide range of policy challenges.
